# SUPERMIND — AI PROMPT GUIDELINE v1
*Paste-ready brand context for any image or video generation prompt. Built from the SUPERMIND World Bible Edition 02 (Keys, Aug 27 2026), the David cinematography reference, and B. Amechi's casting direction. Internal use.*

---

## HOW TO USE
Write your scene idea in one or two sentences ("a woman preparing cacao before sunrise"), then paste the relevant blocks below after it. Always include: THE WORLD + CASTING + COLOR & LIGHT + one VISUAL MODE + NEGATIVE PROMPT. Add CINEMATOGRAPHY for video.

---

## THE WORLD (paste in every prompt)
SUPERMIND is cognitive luxury made tangible — a Black-led wellness house where composed intelligence is the highest form of status. Ancestral warmth and future precision coexist in one civilization: warm archive coffeehouses, luxury laboratory product scenes, plant-filled living intelligence, and ritual in motion (hikes, training, cacao ceremonies, bonfires, writing). The scene must look physically photographable — real light, real materials, real ecology. The world remains recognizable even when the product is absent. Quiet authority: restraint, precision and space create presence. No hype, no chaos, no costume nostalgia.

## CASTING (paste whenever people appear)
Black, African, African-American, mixed and brown men and women, ages 18–40 (all clearly adult), in shape, attractive and put together — masculine men, feminine women, magnetic without being crude. They read as thinkers, builders, athletes, artists and hosts: intelligent, healthy, socially connected, aspirational. Natural skin texture, real hair (locs, coils, waves, fades), no artificial smoothing. Composed expressions — relaxed authority, not excitement. Wardrobe: tailoring, knitwear, natural fibers, quiet technical activewear in black, ivory, tobacco, oxblood, charcoal, dark green; restrained gold or silver jewelry; no fluorescent color, no loud prints, no wellness-uniform costume. For heritage/archive scenes only, elders may appear as part of intergenerational Black intellectual life — dignified, central, never decorative.

## COLOR & LIGHT (paste in every prompt)
Master palette: Black Cherry #55100D (house color, expressed as oxblood material), Coffee Bean #1A0706, Cosmic Black #111111, Signal Ivory #F1EEE7, Alabaster Grey #D9D9D9, Smoked Green #394439, Living Green #536B45, Racing Red #DD0200 (tiny signal accent only). Metals: Crown Satin Gold (large surfaces) with Crown Mirror Gold accents, Mirror Silver for precision — when gold and silver share a scene one owns ~70% of the metallic presence. Gold means inheritance, silver means precision, black cherry means house identity. Light: one committed source — warm amber practicals (3200–4200K) in archive scenes, natural daylight in living scenes, controlled high-CRI soft sources for product. Shadows lean warm brown/burgundy, never gray; blacks stay matte and retain detail; highlights warm and cream-toned; every reflection must explain its source. Never a blanket orange filter, never neon, never flat even lighting.

## VISUAL MODES (pick one per prompt)
**ANALOG ARCHIVE** — the emotional center. Warm coffeehouses, libraries, writing tables, kitchens, records, garden chess, salons, bonfires. Honest 35mm character, 35/50mm lens, warm practicals, readable brown shadows, visible grain. Black intellectual life gathered around thought.
**LUXURY LABORATORY** — product as precise cognitive artifact. Warm gold, mirror silver, smoked green, stone, marble, clear glass. 70–100mm or macro, f8–f11, controlled highlights, revealed seams/grain/embossing, every edge and label legible.
**LIVING INTELLIGENCE** — plants as cultivation, not decoration. Botanical coffeehouses, greenhouse research, ingredient cultivation, mature trees, natural daylight, believable greens (cacao, coffee, olive, fig, oak, ficus, palms, ferns, herbs). Never a decorative jungle.
**RITUAL IN MOTION** — the lifestyle engine. Hikes at golden hour, gym strength work, cacao preparation, gathering at long tables, journaling, bonfires at dusk. 28/35/50mm, natural directional light, fast shutter, real motion, product inside the action rather than interrupting it.

## CINEMATOGRAPHY (paste for video / motion)
Slow-paced and dramatic in the David register: chiaroscuro lighting, one hard committed source, deep readable shadows, deliberate composition, long holds punctuated by one decisive cut on the reveal. Camera locked or gliding — never shaky, never whip-panned. Speed ramps reserved for drop films. Sound design: ritual foley forward (the pour, the stir, the cup on stone, pen on paper, fire crackle) under slow classical strings or sparse jazz — unhurried, dramatic, composed. The film is about how the moment feels, not what the product does. 15–30s drop films: one product, one line of copy, dramatic light, nothing else.

## PRODUCT RULES (paste when product appears)
Product may be hero (launch/material stories), tool (inside a real ritual), evidence (naturally present), or absent — the behavior carries the brand. Packaging: oxblood/black-cherry metallic pouches and wrappers with centered SUPERMIND wordmark and quiet vertical hierarchy; gold family for ceremony; mirror silver family for performance. Reusable vessels are the visible standard: gold (ceremonial), silver (performance), oxblood ceramic (coffeehouse), clear glass (water). Never generic cups, disposable plastic bottles, or third-party branded vessels. Never imply an ingredient that is not in the product.

## NEGATIVE PROMPT (paste in every prompt)
No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermarks. No neon, no pastels, no blanket orange cast, no crushed blacks, no flat lighting, no gray shadows. No sepia nostalgia filter, no costume reenactment. No plastic foliage, fake plant walls, fantasy ecosystems. No mascots, exotic animals or staged wildlife. No alcohol, tobacco, intoxication. No influencer poses, sterile biohacker aesthetics, artificial skin smoothing, or generic wellness-brand brightness. No stretched/retyped wordmark, no unapproved symbols. No one who reads as a minor. No clutter — one intentional idea per frame.
